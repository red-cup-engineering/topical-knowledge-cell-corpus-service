# Topical Knowledge Cell Corpus Service

This settlement owns one bounded faculty: it validates a customer-owned topical
knowledge-cell definition and forms a receipt-bound reconciliation intent for a
separately hired notebook provider. It never creates or mutates a notebook.

The caller owns the corpus sources and disclosure decision. The service owns
only its law, offers, resource account, reconciliation decisions, refusals, and
provider-local witness history. RMN identity and RWIL/RDF durability cross
their independently hired provider boundaries; tests may inject doubles but
production may not replace those providers with private implementations.

Nation-, colony-, Union-, and public-scope cells are explicitly
non-authoritative projections. No result from this settlement is a central
Union view or grants creation, upload, deletion, sharing, publication, report,
payment, or customer-territory authority.
